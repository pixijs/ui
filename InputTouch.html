Class: InputTouch

InputTouch

Fourth layer of Input: presses on the hidden field itself, on touch devices. The field has to stay the touch target there, because its long-press callout is the only way to paste on iOS, so taps, multi-taps and drags arrive on it rather than on the canvas and are mapped onto the drawn text here. Not meant to be used on its own; Input is the component.
